Jane Jacobs' iconic book, The Death and Life of Great American Cities, can be purchased locally in the Claremont area at independent shops like TBR Books, the Claremont Forum Bookshop, or the Huntley Bookstore of the Claremont Colleges. For free borrowing and digital rentals, residents and students can access the book through the Claremont Helen Renwick Library or The Claremont Colleges Library using platforms like Libby. If you prefer to shop or rent from home, major online marketplaces such as Amazon, Barnes and Noble, and Chegg offer immediate digital access as well as physical copies. Additionally, readers looking to support independent businesses online can order the book directly through Bookshop.org.
